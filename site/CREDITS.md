## About page (full)

**Data sources**

- **OpenF1** ([openf1.org](https://openf1.org)): session, lap, stint, pit, position, overtake,
  race control and weather data, used under the
  [Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/)
  licence. Non-commercial use only; data derived from OpenF1 is shared under the same licence.
- **F1DB** ([github.com/f1db/f1db](https://github.com/f1db/f1db)): historical results and
  records since 1950, used under the
  [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) licence.
- **Jolpica F1 API** ([api.jolpi.ca](https://api.jolpi.ca), [github.com/jolpica/jolpica-f1](https://github.com/jolpica/jolpica-f1)):
  results, qualifying, standings, lap times, pit stops and the race calendar. Jolpica is the
  community-run successor of the Ergast Developer API; thanks to both projects.
- **FastF1** ([github.com/theOehrly/Fast-F1](https://github.com/theOehrly/Fast-F1), MIT licence):
  laps, tyres, sector times and car positions, read from Formula 1's public live timing archive.
- **Formula 1 live timing** (livetiming.formula1.com): the free live timing feed and its public
  archive power the live page and replays. Car positions during a live session are estimated
  from the timing feed when no official positions are available.
- **DHL Fastest Pit Stop Award** ([inmotion.dhl](https://inmotion.dhl/en/formula-1/fastest-pit-stop-award)):
  pit stop times, used to reconstruct fantasy pit stop points.
- **F1 Fantasy** (fantasy.formula1.com): prices and points from the official game's public feeds.
  Overtakes, Driver of the Day and pit stop bands are reconstructed from free data, so fantasy
  points shown here can differ from the official game (typically by about 2.6 points per driver
  and 4 points per constructor per race).
- **MultiViewer** ([multiviewer.app](https://multiviewer.app)): circuit outlines, corner numbers
  and map rotation.
